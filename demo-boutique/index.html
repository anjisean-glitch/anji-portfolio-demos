Our Story

Style Born in
Lagos, Living
in Port Harcourt.

Zara Lagos Boutique was founded in 2018 by Zara Adeyemi, a Lagos-trained fashion designer who moved to Port Harcourt and couldn't find a boutique that matched her taste. So she built one.

We stock only premium, curated pieces — from contemporary ready-to-wear to full traditional Aso-Oke and bespoke bridal. Every item is personally selected or made in our studio.

Quality First

We never compromise on fabric, finish or fit. Only premium makes the cut.

Made for You

Bespoke and made-to-measure options for every occasion and size.

Fast Delivery

Same-day delivery within Port Harcourt on ready-to-wear orders.

Easy Returns

Not happy? We'll fix it or exchange it. No stress, no drama.
