Cooking with Soul since 2013

The Naija Kitchen was born from a simple belief: that Nigerian food deserves to be celebrated. We started as a small chop-bar in Rumuola and grew into Port Harcourt's most beloved authentic restaurant.

Every recipe is passed down from our grandmother's kitchen. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just real, honest food made the way it's always been done.
