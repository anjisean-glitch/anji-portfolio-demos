# Anji Portfolio Demos

A collection of website design demonstrations built to explore how different businesses can use websites to present their services, connect with customers, and establish an online presence.

The repository includes four business website concepts and a separate portfolio website.

## Projects

### 1. Zara Lagos Boutique
A fashion retail website concept designed to showcase clothing, communicate brand identity, and direct customers to WhatsApp.

[View Source](demo-boutique/index.html) | [Live Demo](https://zaralagosboutique.vercel.app/)

### 2. LifeCare Medical Centre
A healthcare website concept presenting medical services and providing an appointment contact option.

[View Source](demo-clinic/index.html) | [Live Demo](https://lifecare-medical-ph.vercel.app/)

### 3. The Naija Kitchen
A restaurant website concept showcasing Nigerian cuisine, menu information, and customer contact options.

[View Source](demo-restaurant/index.html) | [Live Demo](https://naijakitchen-ph.vercel.app/)

### 4. Glam & Grace Salon
A beauty salon website concept presenting services and providing appointment contact options.

[View Source](demo-salon/index.html) | [Live Demo](https://glamandgrace-ph.vercel.app/)

## Purpose

These projects demonstrate practical website design concepts across different business categories, with an emphasis on visual presentation, business communication, and customer interaction.

They are demonstration projects rather than representations of confirmed client engagements.
